CH.05 — DUSK / THE DRIVE BEHIND IT

I.

Inspired by the old school

My creative drive comes from the media and interactive software of decades ago — the games, demos and tools that made me want to build. Modern technology lets me explore and expand those ideas in rapid, scalable, deployable ways: straight from a spark on a page into something you can run.

II.

From idea to game-ready asset

I visualize and conceptualize ideas directly into 3D, game-ready assets or partner with art directors to shape and deliver them. I organize and ship clean Unity asset packages that plug straight into a pipeline on day one.

III.

Tools that build worlds — privately

I also write the add-ons and scripting behind modular level design and world building. I run local models like Qwen 3.8 27B. Slower, yes; but no outages, no token limits, no extra compute fees and everything stays local and private.

IV.

The art department, without the cost

A professional 3D artist / Generalist for over a decade and a self-taught C#/Unity developer for about 5 years, I can articulate the steps of complex 3D systems in ways only a seasoned 3D Generalist can, while framing them so the models interpret them correctly. The result: working add-ons, scripts, Blender modifiers, tools, Unity mechanics… just about anything. A creative powerhouse waiting for a small team that wants to build a game and needs an art department — without the cost.
